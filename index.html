Overview

Construction is a field that has created all the buildings and furniture you see every day. Technology in construction is progressing rapidly in this century. This website highlights apps, tools, and hardware that improve construction work.

Some examples include websites like Dusty Robotics, which focuses on robotics, 3D printing, and drones, and DPM, which features tools like Contractor Foreman, monday.com, and Crew Cost.

Existing Technology

Contractor Tools

Contractor Foreman

  • Paid service
  • Helps manage estimates, expenses, and bills
  • Includes calendars and document templates

Crew Cost

  • Project management tool
  • Tracks profits and expenses
  • Designed specifically for construction

monday.com

  • Free and simple to use
  • Organizes tasks
  • Supports collaboration

Construction Workers

3D Printing

  • Reduces cost and time
  • Creates complex designs
  • Builds parts and furniture

Robotics

  • Used for demolition and excavation
  • Improves safety
  • Reduces manual labor

Inspectors

Drones

  • Inspect sites safely
  • Reach small or dangerous areas
  • Increase efficiency

Future Inventions

Extending Boots

Boots that help workers reach high places safely without ladders.

AI Managers

AI systems that manage taxes, estimates, and organization automatically.

Holographic Projection

Displays building designs in real space to detect flaws before construction.

Self-Healing Buildings

Buildings that repair themselves, reducing costs and increasing safety.

Nanotech Armor

Advanced protective gear that prevents injuries using nanotechnology.
